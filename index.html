About

Making one converter serve three ports efficiently, across every load and voltage it will actually see.

I am a doctoral researcher in power electronics at IIT Bhubaneswar. My work focuses on the triple active bridge: how to choose its phase shifts so the converter keeps soft switching, minimizes circulating current and regulates each port independently.

Research

What I'm working on

MODULATION

Multi-phase-shift control

Choosing inner and outer phase shifts across three bridges to cut RMS current and widen the zero-voltage-switching range.

OPTIMIZATION

Loss-minimizing operation

Writing converter losses as an objective and solving for the best operating point over the full power range.

POWER FLOW

Decoupled port control

Handling the cross-coupling between ports so each port's power can be regulated on its own.
